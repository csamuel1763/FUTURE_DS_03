# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior across an e-commerce marketing funnel using real-world user interaction data. The objective is to understand how customers progress from viewing products to making purchases, identify conversion bottlenecks, evaluate product performance, and generate actionable business recommendations.

The analysis combines Python-based exploratory data analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard to provide insights into customer engagement, conversion performance, and revenue generation.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses receive millions of customer interactions daily. Understanding how users move through the sales funnel is essential for improving conversion rates and maximizing revenue.

This project answers key business questions:

* How many users progress from product views to purchases?
* Where do customers drop off in the funnel?
* Which brands and categories generate the highest revenue?
* How can conversion rates be improved?
* Which products should receive more marketing focus?

---

**Dataset:** E-Commerce Behavior Data from Multi-Category Store

**Period:** November 2019

**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset contains millions of customer interaction records including:

## Key Insights

### Customer Funnel

* Most user interactions occur at the View stage.
* Significant customer drop-off occurs before products are added to the cart.
* Overall conversion rate remains relatively low compared to total traffic.

### Revenue Insights

* Smartphone products generate the highest revenue.
* Revenue is concentrated among a few premium product categories.
* A small number of brands contribute a significant portion of total revenue.

### Product Insights

* Apple is the leading revenue-generating brand.
* Premium electronic products contribute the majority of sales value.
* High-performing categories present strong opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.

---

## Business Recommendations

### Improve Product Page Engagement

Enhance product descriptions, images, ratings, and customer reviews to encourage cart additions.

### Focus on High-Revenue Categories

Allocate more marketing resources toward top-performing product categories.

### Expand Premium Brand Campaigns

Develop targeted campaigns for brands generating the highest revenue.

### Optimize Conversion Funnel

Implement strategies to reduce customer drop-off and improve overall conversion performance.
